Privacy Policy for Forest Cabin

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Forest Cabin is developed and published by Mezhenon Production. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the game.

1. Information We Collect

Forest Cabin does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address.

Game progress, settings, and other gameplay data are stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising

Forest Cabin may display optional rewarded advertisements using Google AdMob. You may choose to watch a rewarded advertisement in exchange for an in-game benefit.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, approximate location, advertising interactions, and diagnostic data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

For users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and other regions where required, the game may use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent before personalized advertising or related data processing.

You can learn more about Google's privacy practices at: Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-App Purchases

Forest Cabin may offer an optional Premium purchase that removes advertising and provides the same rewarded benefits without requiring the player to watch an advertisement.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. Mezhenon Production does not receive or store your credit card number, banking information, or other payment credentials.

Google Play may process purchase information according to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

4. Purchase Restoration

If you purchase Premium, the game may use Google Play Billing to verify ownership of the purchase and restore your Premium entitlement after reinstalling the game or using another compatible device connected to the same Google Play account.

5. Local Game Data

Forest Cabin stores game progress and settings locally on your device. Deleting the application or clearing its application data may remove locally stored game progress and settings.

Premium purchase ownership may be restored separately through Google Play, provided the purchase is still associated with your Google Play account.

6. Third-Party Services

Forest Cabin may use third-party services including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)

These services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Age Requirement and Children's Privacy

Forest Cabin is intended for users aged 18 and over and is not directed to children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that personal information from a child has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it where applicable.

Third-party services used by the game may process data according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to avoid collecting unnecessary personal information. However, no digital service can guarantee complete security.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the game, its features, or third-party services change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
